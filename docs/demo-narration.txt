CredIntel AI is an explainable underwriting workspace for new-to-credit applicants. It combines consented alternative data with separate repayment, fraud, and policy controls.

The first scenario is approved. The analyst sees the recommendation, credit confidence, fraud risk, decision latency, and the strongest reason codes in one reviewable record.

Opening the explanation reveals the exact feature contributions and policy checks. The language model can summarize this evidence, but it cannot change the score or recommendation.

A borderline affordability signal routes the next application to human review, even with strong repayment evidence.

In the fraud scenario, low device trust, identity inconsistency, location changes, and failed logins drive a decline. The displayed reasons stay faithful to that decision.

The review queue prioritizes cases for analysts. Model monitoring exposes validation, drift, fairness, and governance status. Finally, the audit trail records each data, model, policy, and human event.

CredIntel demonstrates a working path from responsible credit expansion to a controlled production pilot.
